# VPL principles

1. **Source before invention.** Requirements come from the ticket, call, roadmap, or prototype. When sources disagree, the latest wins and the conflict gets flagged. Mark anything inferred.
2. **Originals are sacred.** Approved pages and working files are read-only by default. Duplicate into Post MVP or a labelled section. Scripts that mutate a copy start with a guard against the original.
3. **Know the phase.** The project's phase decides what's in scope. See the table in `SKILL.md`.
4. **System first.** Reuse the file's components and icons. Bind text, colour, and numbers to variables. New components follow the existing set's level of detail.
5. **Measure, don't eyeball.** Claims about a design come from the real frame: a lint result, a measurement, or a screenshot you looked at. Say what you didn't check.
6. **Cover every state.** A flow isn't finished at its happy path. Check empty, error, loading, first-run, and destructive states.
7. **Fewer screens, more scenarios.** Merge steps that are mostly empty space. Keep key actions visible and put destructive ones behind a menu.
8. **Ask once.** Look things up before asking. Combine what's left into one approval card or one question with a recommendation. State assumptions and keep moving on reversible work.
9. **Show only what matters.** Lead with what needs a decision. Raise a repeated issue once and name where else it applies.
10. **Be a partner.** Give your own recommendation, and push back with a reason and a better option when an idea is weaker. Flag requests that look meant for another project.
11. **Every repeated note becomes a check.** Encode it in a script first, a playbook second.
