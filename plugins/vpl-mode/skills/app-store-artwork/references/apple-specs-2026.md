## Technical Requirements

- **Format:** PNG (preferred) or JPEG
- **Color space:** sRGB (not Display P3, not Adobe RGB)
- **Transparency:** NOT allowed — solid backgrounds only
- **Orientation:** Portrait for portrait apps, landscape for landscape-only apps
- **Max file size:** 10MB per screenshot
- **Max screenshots:** 10 per device slot per locale
- **Minimum:** 1 per device slot

---

## Content Rules (Apple Review Guidelines 2.3.1)

✅ Allowed:
- Custom background colors and graphic treatments
- Text overlays and headlines
- Device frames/mockups around real app UI
- Decorative elements (shapes, gradients, patterns)
- Marketing-polished versions of real screens
- Lifestyle imagery combined with device frames

❌ Not allowed / rejection triggers:
- Screenshots that show features not in the binary
- Fake reviews or ratings (showing 5-star badges not awarded)
- Award claims not backed by real awards
- Competitor comparisons
- Misleading UI (showing dark mode if app is light-only, etc.)
- Screens that misrepresent the actual user experience
- Content not in the app (e.g. "coming soon" features)

## Positioning in App Store

- **Search results:** Shows first 1–3 screenshots in a horizontal strip
- **Product page:** Shows all screenshots in a scrollable gallery
- **First screenshot = most critical** — it's the only one seen in many search contexts
- Screenshots update independently from app binary (24–48hr review for screenshot-only changes)

---

## Safe Zones (Critical for All Sizes)

Keep all critical text and UI elements away from:
- **Dynamic Island area** (6.9" and newer): Top 80px of screen area inside device frame
- **Home indicator** (all non-home-button iPhones): Bottom 34px of screen area
- **Physical home button** (5.5" / 8 Plus): Bottom ~100px of device — this is hardware, not screen
- **Artboard edges:** Minimum 60px margin from any edge of the artboard
- **Rounded corners:** Account for ~50px corner radius on device frames

---

## Status Bar Convention

For marketing screenshots, Apple's canonical values:
- **Time:** 9:41 AM (matches Apple's product marketing convention)
- **Carrier:** Hide or show full signal (no carrier name)
- **Battery:** Full (100%)
- **WiFi:** Full signal

Easiest approach: hide the status bar entirely in the Figma screen — clean and universally accepted.
