# Google Play store listing graphics

Last checked: November 2026, from a third-party 2026 guide and Google's own developer blog, not the Play Console help page. **Check the Play Console's current requirements before exporting.**

| Asset | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Phone screenshots | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) | Team default. 1440 × 2560 also works. 2 to 8 per listing. Each side 320 to 3840 px, and the long side no more than twice the short side |
| Feature graphic | 1024 × 500 | Required. Shown at the top of the listing and in promotions. No device, just brand, logo and one short line. Keep key content away from the edges, since Play can crop or overlay it |
| App icon | 512 × 512 | Usually comes from the app icon page, not this skill |

**Format:** PNG or JPEG, no transparency.

## Android, not iPhone
- Never put Play screenshots in an iPhone mockup. Use an Android phone mockup if the file or a published library has one. Otherwise use a frameless screen with rounded corners and a soft shadow.
- If the HiFi screens are iOS (Dynamic Island, iOS status bar), swap in an Android status bar when the file has one. Otherwise crop the status bar off. Say which you did.
- Don't show Apple-only features such as Apple Pay. Use the Android equivalent if the app supports it (Google Pay), or leave it out.

## Recomposing from the 6.9" set
1080 × 1920 is wider for its height than the iPhone frames (0.5625 against 0.46). Re-space rather than scale: keep the headline block, shrink the device to fit, and tighten the gap between headline and phone, as for 5.5".
