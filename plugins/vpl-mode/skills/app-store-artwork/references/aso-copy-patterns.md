# ASO Copy Patterns — Headline Frameworks

Proven headline structures that drive App Store conversions. Always adapt to the specific app's brand voice and user outcomes — never use these templates verbatim.

---

## Headline Structures That Convert

### 1. The Outcome Statement
**Pattern:** [Feeling/Result] you've always wanted
**Examples:**
- "Connections That Feel Real"
- "A Space to Feel Seen"
- "Gaming Without Limits"

Best for: Social, wellness, lifestyle, creative apps

---

### 2. The Empowerment Verb
**Pattern:** [Strong verb] + [object/context]
**Examples:**
- "Find Your People"
- "Track Every Catch"
- "Master Your Money"

Best for: Utility, productivity, sports/fitness apps

---

### 3. The Problem Inversion
**Pattern:** No more [pain point] / Never [negative thing] again
**Examples:**
- "Never Miss a Match"
- "No More App Switching"
- "Stop Forgetting Everything"

Best for: Productivity, scheduling, reminder apps

---

### 4. The Aspiration Statement
**Pattern:** [Who you become] with this app
**Examples:**
- "Play Like a Pro"
- "Cook Like a Chef"
- "Run Your Business Smarter"

Best for: Skill-building, education, professional apps

---

### 5. The Specificity Hook
**Pattern:** [Specific metric/detail] that matters
**Examples:**
- "Track Every Fish. Raised, Hooked or Caught."
- "Your Legacy, One Log at a Time"
- "Court Matches. No Hassle."

Best for: Niche apps with specific, passionate user bases

---

### 6. The Emotional Promise
**Pattern:** [Feeling state] starts here
**Examples:**
- "Where Real Connection Begins"
- "Your Calm Starts Here"
- "Joy. Daily."

Best for: Wellness, meditation, lifestyle apps

## App Category Guidance

### Social / Community Apps
- Lead with the human connection, not the feature
- Words that work: "real", "genuine", "meaningful", "people who get you"
- Avoid: "network", "platform", "users", "connect with strangers"
- Screenshot order: Explore/discover → Message/connect → Events/activities → Profile

### Sports / Niche Activity Apps
- Lead with the passion of the activity, not the logging/tracking
- Use the vocabulary of the community (fishing: "raised/hooked/caught", not "logged/recorded")
- Show data-rich screens — the more detailed, the more credible
- Screenshot order: Dashboard/summary → Core activity → AI/smart features → Data/insights

### Productivity / Scheduling Apps
- Lead with time saved or stress reduced
- Show the "after" state (organized, clear, on top of things)
- Words that work: "instantly", "effortlessly", "at a glance", "one tap"
- Screenshot order: Main view → Key action/feature → Notification/reminder → Detail view

## Sub-headline Patterns

Sub-headlines work best when they answer "how" or add specificity to the main headline:

| Headline | Sub-headline |
|---|---|
| "Find Your People" | "Filter by interests, location, and vibe" |
| "Track Every Catch" | "Raised, hooked, or caught — all in one log" |
| "Let's Play Tennis" | "Invite players. Book courts. Show up." |
| "A Space to Feel Seen" | "Real conversations with people who get it" |

Keep sub-headlines under 8 words. If it needs more words, the headline isn't specific enough.

---

## Copy Anti-Patterns (Avoid These)

❌ **Feature-first copy**
- "Advanced filtering system with 12 options" → ✅ "Find exactly who you're looking for"
- "AI-powered suggestion engine" → ✅ "Smart matches, effortlessly"

❌ **Passive voice**
- "Be connected to others" → ✅ "Connect with people who get you"
- "Logs are created automatically" → ✅ "Your log writes itself"

❌ **Vague aspirations without grounding**
- "The best app ever" → Not allowed (Apple)
- "Amazing experience" → ✅ "Chat that feels natural"

❌ **Too many words**
- "Discover a new way to connect with like-minded people in your area" → ✅ "Find Your People"

❌ **All lowercase without intentionality**
- Random lowercase can feel unpolished unless it's clearly a brand style choice

## Readability Checks

Before finalizing headlines, verify:
- [ ] Readable at 25% zoom (thumbnail test)
- [ ] No more than 6 words in main headline
- [ ] No jargon that non-users wouldn't understand
- [ ] Consistent tone across all screenshots in the set
- [ ] First word is strong (action verb or emotionally resonant noun)
- [ ] Passes the "so what?" test — does it explain why I should care?
